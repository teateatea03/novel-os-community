# Code of Conduct

English | [繁體中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-TW.md)

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, intellectual property, and content boundaries.
- Use synthetic examples instead of exposing private creative work or personal data.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect safety, privacy, provenance, or project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention directed at contributors, doxxing, publishing private communications, deliberate credential exposure, plagiarism, or repeated attempts to bypass project privacy and safety controls are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or access that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Reports should be made privately to the repository owner until a dedicated conduct address is published.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Attribution

This is a concise project-specific conduct policy. Before public launch, maintainers may replace it with a formally adopted community standard and its required attribution text.
