- `subject_category`: `real`／`novel`／`anime`／`film`／`other`
- `subject_subcategory`
- `subject_kind`
- `source_medium`
- `canon_scope`
- `version_scope`
- `franchise`
- `classification_confidence`

真人／歷史人物、小說、動漫、電影版本與其他媒介不可在未標註的情況下混用。分類契約以 `../novel-character-deep-digger/references/character-category-taxonomy.md` 為準；資料庫對應欄位見 `../character-database-builder/references/character-category-schema.md`。
